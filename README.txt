THE ONE THING YOU MUST FILL IN

Your six links. They are all in ONE place, together, at the end of
assets/js/data.js — look for "contact".

   Replace:  REPLACE_FACEBOOK_URL     with your Facebook address
             REPLACE_INSTAGRAM_URL    with your Instagram address
             REPLACE_LINKEDIN_URL     with your LinkedIn address
             REPLACE_BEHANCE_URL      with your Behance address
             REPLACE@EXAMPLE.COM      with your email address
             REPLACE_WHATSAPP_URL     with your WhatsApp Business link

Keep the quote marks around each one. Save, refresh the browser.

Until you do, each portal shows "ADD LINK" instead of "OPEN", and a lime
note appears on the Connection page so you cannot forget. Delete that
note (search for "To complete" in assets/js/app.js) once you are done.

Nothing was invented on your behalf — no links, no clients, no awards.

HOW IT IS BUILT

THREE ACTS, not a navbar:
   I   THE AUTHOR      you, the eight-frame campaign, biography, tools
   II  THE WORK        the Atlas — 12 fields, 38 projects, 260 images
   III THE CONNECTION  the six portals and the closing signature

THE SIGNATURE INTERACTION — "THE CUT"
   On the opening portrait and on every project, two images meet along a
   diagonal taken from your own M. Drag left and right across it: the
   images trade places and the name is physically split by the seam,
   half on each side. Arrow keys work too, so it is not mouse-only.
   It is the one move that should make people try it twice.

WORD CHOICE
   The twelve groupings are called FIELDS, and the whole body of work is
   THE ATLAS. The word "Worlds" appears nowhere.

HONESTY
   All 37 brand projects are labelled "Speculative project" on their own
   page and in the Atlas introduction. Project 00 ACADEMIA is labelled
   "Established campaign". No education, school or diploma is mentioned
   anywhere. No fake clients, testimonials, awards or statistics.

IMAGES

Your 268 originals were about 780 MB — far too heavy for a website.
Each was re-encoded into three sizes (480 / 1200 / 2000 px) as WebP, so
the browser downloads only what the visitor's screen needs.

Nothing was cropped, stretched, filtered or colour-graded. The five
ratios (9:16, 16:9, 3:4, 4:3, 1:1) are preserved exactly, and every
original filename is kept inside assets/img/.

KEEP YOUR ORIGINAL FILES. This folder holds web-sized copies only.
