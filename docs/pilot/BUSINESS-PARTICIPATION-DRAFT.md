**MARKYRA — Pilot participation**

MARKYRA is a map where people in Grand Tunis discover local businesses. We are
running a pilot with a small number of businesses.

**What would appear publicly**
Business name · category · address · location on the map · opening hours (if you
give them) · phone number (only with your agreement) · website or page (if you
have one) · photos (if you provide them).

**What you can do**
Claim the listing by receiving a code on the number shown, and then correct
anything that is wrong. Ask us to remove the listing at any time — we act within
five working days.

**What "claimed" and "verified" mean**
*Claimed* means you proved you control the listed number. *Verified* means we
checked a document or visited. **Claiming is not verification**, and neither is
paid for.

**During the pilot**
Everything is free. Nothing is guaranteed. We do not promise customers, traffic,
revenue, or any position in results. We are testing whether this is useful.
